RESEARCH RULE 01

Every idea needs a reason.

Define who is on the other side of the trade, why the price inefficiency might exist, when it should disappear and what would disprove the thesis.

RESEARCH RULE 02

The holdout stays sealed.

Keep evaluation periods independent of strategy selection. Record every tested configuration—including failures—to reduce selection bias.

# Strategy Research v0.9 hypothesis review

## A. Volatility-adjusted trend breakout

**Priority 1.** Trend persistence is a plausible source of gross returns. A price breakout through prior highs and a slower exit through prior lows separate entry from exit, which could reduce the rapid re-entry seen in v0.8. Sizing against ATR assigns fewer units when the stop is wider. Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen found time-series momentum across diversified futures at considerably longer horizons. Liu and Tsyvinski document time-series momentum in cryptocurrency. Moreira and Muir provide broader evidence for volatility management. Those studies motivate separate hypotheses about persistence and sizing. They leave this specific four-hour/daily spot rule, its turnover and its execution costs to be tested. QuantConnect's open-source LEAN indicator is a useful reference for rolling highs and lows. [Time series momentum](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003) · [Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa113) · [Volatility-Managed Portfolios](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12513) · [LEAN Donchian implementation](https://github.com/QuantConnect/Lean/blob/master/Indicators/DonchianChannel.cs)

Required data are aligned spot OHLCV plus quotes and depth before execution can be assessed. Research implementation complexity is low to moderate. Sideways whipsaws, gaps beyond the planned stop, correlated breakouts and persistent underexposure can defeat the thesis. Reject the registered variant when net economics, cost stress, consistency, drawdown or matched benchmark hurdles fail. The test compares a four-hour trigger with daily confirmation against a daily trigger with weekly confirmation and includes one predeclared lookback neighbour per timeframe.

## B. Regime-conditional mean reversion

**Priority 2.** Temporary liquidity demand can push prices away from a recent centre. A rapid rebound could make a spot long viable if it is large enough to cover both sides of execution. Zaremba et al. link daily cross-sectional reversals to less liquid assets and find that the most tradeable coins often exhibit momentum. That is direct counterevidence to a universal major-coin reversal rule. The candidate instead tests a four-hour, ATR-normalised downside deviation within a ten-day range, with a centre, range-break or 24-hour exit. Required data are hourly OHLCV for feasibility and historical quotes/trades for filtering bid/ask bounce and modelling queue and latency. Implementation complexity is moderate. Trend continuation, spread widening and insufficient net payoff are the main failure modes. Reject if the after-cost thresholds fail. [Short-term reversal, momentum and liquidity](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2021.101908)

## C. Relative-strength market rotation

**Exploratory.** Relative strength could direct the portfolio towards the strongest member or cash during broad weakness. Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu identify market, size and momentum factors in broader crypto cross-sections. Three correlated majors offer little cross-sectional breadth. Re-ranking can increase costs and concentrate the same market beta. A future test needs simultaneous candles, point-in-time universe rules, realistic rebalance costs, a cash hurdle and a beta/exposure-matched equal-weight comparison. Complexity is moderate. Falsify the thesis if relative performance disappears after common-factor adjustment and costs. No rotation variant is registered in this release. [Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13119)

## D. Funding and basis carry

**Theoretical.** Convergence of a dated futures basis or receipt of floating perpetual funding can compensate spot/futures inventory and margin risk. BIS research identifies substantial, time-varying BTC and ETH carry and limits to arbitrage, including margin and regulatory frictions. Quoted basis and annualised funding are not equivalent to realised carry. A feasibility study needs timestamped spot and derivatives quotes, actual funding and expiry rules, collateral haircuts, contract and margin schedules, fee and financing terms, as well as counterparty and venue eligibility. Complexity is high. Funding reversals, margin calls, liquidation, settlement disruption, collateral depegs and venue failures can erase theoretical carry. Reject if fully costed cash-and-carry loses its margin or fails collateral stress. ASIC's 2023 cancellation was for the named Australian derivatives licensee. Current eligibility for any venue, instrument or account must be established separately. [BIS Crypto Carry](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry) · [ASIC licence notice](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2023-releases/23-091mr-binance-australia-derivatives-afs-licence-cancelled)

## Execution economics and evidence limits

- Binance publishes 10 bps maker and taker for its regular-user spot tier. Actual commission can vary with account and symbol, promotions, tax and fee asset. Historical spreads, order-book depth, executable slippage, rejections, minimum increments, fill fractions and latency are unobserved for these inputs. The experiment holds the modelled 10 bps fee and 10 bps adverse concession per side, includes a doubled-friction case and delays discretionary signals by one hour in a separate stress. Hourly bar volume does not establish executable liquidity. Calibration requires depth and top-of-book snapshots synchronised with trades, fee eligibility verified outside research credentials, and a size-dependent capacity and latency study. Cash-only spot has no modelled borrowing, funding or stablecoin yield. Conversion, taxes and collateral costs are outside the model. [Binance spot fees](https://www.binance.com/en/fee/trading) · [Commission FAQ](https://github.com/binance/binance-spot-api-docs/blob/master/faqs/commission_faq.md) · [Archive specification](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data)
- The model uses time-truncated signal checks and an independent replay. Freqtrade's lookahead documentation illustrates why full-frame computation and portfolio assumptions merit separate testing. Daily paired block-bootstrap intervals are descriptive because the research data were previously examined, the number of independent regimes is limited and the three assets share market factors. The fixed comparison family is Bonferroni-adjusted. [Freqtrade lookahead analysis](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/latest/lookahead-analysis/) · [Politis and Romano on dependent resampling](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870)
- Keep AI outside this experiment and the risk decision path. Research support or classification would require its own frozen ablation against these simpler deterministic strategies before any integration review.

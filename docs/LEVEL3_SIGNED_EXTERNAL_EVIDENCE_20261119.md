- Receipt describes a custody transaction, not an audit-event chain head; no full chain or cross-checkpoint ancestry was proven. **Do not mark full_chain_verification=true.**
- The signing private key is ephemeral, so this key cannot support an operational series of future checkpoints. A durable auditor-controlled KMS/HSM key, non-exportable signing policy, audited retention and continuity checks are required.
- No live trading or production release is authorised by this proof.

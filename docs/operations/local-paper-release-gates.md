# Local paper release gates

**CONDITIONAL GO for limited local simulated research.** Use a fresh disposable journal or a separately verified working copy. The default Coinbase profile uses observed public BTC-USD, ETH-USD and SOL-USD books. The synthetic ETF profile runs in its own journal. Its data source and calendar are rehearsal fixtures. Limit the Coinbase profile to permitted personal or internal research and verify provider rights before redistribution. [Coinbase market-data terms](https://www.coinbase.com/en-au/legal/market_data)

| Gate | Decision |
|---|---|
| Main-based scope and deterministic risk | Pass on tested Linux source. Keep explicit arming, 0.25% planned loss, 1% aggregate stop-risk, 2% daily loss, 5% drawdown and 1× exposure. |
| Local write and restart integrity | Pass on disposable Linux and historical-schema fixtures. A changed or missing journal requires recovery and a verified working path. |
| Existing Android data | Operator-provided read-only verification and disposable live smoke passed on `0e53854` with Android Python 3.13.13. Backup publication and disposable restore with startup HALT passed on `2928092`. Re-run the storage and risk regressions on the candidate commit. |
| Independent rollback | Local SQLite alone is unanchored. Use separately held receipts for fill verification and independent custody for protected full-state recovery. |
| Live equities and additional symbols | Research-only until permitted data access, instrument metadata, regular sessions, holidays, corporate actions and currency handling are independently verified. |
| Hosted paper | NO-GO. Real Auth0, TLS ingress, tenant store, durable checkpoints and representative restore acceptance remain open. |
| Real money and autonomous strategy | NO-GO. This candidate supports operator-controlled simulation and deterministic protective stops. |

Before merging, require exact-head CI, independent review of storage and risk changes, physical Android verification of backup and recovery on the pinned candidate, and a passed disposable on-device order/restart smoke. Any corruption, unverifiable rollback or failed safety gate blocks the merge decision.

Before staging or broader rollout, independently verify the authenticated hosted boundary and protected storage or keep execution on the approved local profile. Any consequential service change requires its own owner approval. Use only available free resources. No paid hosting or data subscription is required for the limited local scope.
